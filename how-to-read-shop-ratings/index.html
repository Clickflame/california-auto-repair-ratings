A star rating is only half the story. Here is how to read one, using 17,778 rated California shops as the yardstick.

Read The Count Before The Stars

Stars tell you how happy people were. The review count tells you how many people that was. A shop with six reviews can hold a perfect score with ease. A shop with three hundred reviews has met every kind of customer and every kind of bad day.

In our California file, 3,024 shops show a perfect 5.0. Of those, 32 percent have fewer than 10 reviews. The table shows what happens to the average as the count grows.

Know What A Normal Score Looks Like

The average California repair shop in this count holds 4.61 stars, and 76 percent hold 4.5 or more. That means a 4.5 is a normal score, not a rare one. Use it as a floor. Then let the review count, the dates and the words in the reviews do the sorting.

The middle shop in the state has 53 reviews. A shop well above that number has a rating that is hard to fake and hard to sink with one upset customer.

Five Checks That Take Two Minutes

  1. Check the count. Fewer than 10 reviews means the score can swing with one new review.
  2. Sort by newest. A shop can change owners or staff. The last ten reviews matter more than the first ten.
  3. Read the low ones. Look at what went wrong and whether the shop answered.
  4. Match the job. Find reviews that name the same kind of repair you need.
  5. Check your city. See what is normal where you live in the city table.
