What The Table Shows

Most Shops Rate High

76 percent of rated shops in the state sit at 4.5 stars or more. A 4.5 is common, so it should be your floor and not your finish line.

Big Cities Differ

Among cities with 75 or more rated shops, Burbank has the highest average at 4.77 stars. Compton has the lowest at 4.37.

Review Counts Vary A Lot

The middle shop in San Diego has 94 reviews. The middle shop statewide has 53. More reviews make a star rating easier to trust.

Many Shops Have No Website

Only 57 percent of rated shops list a website, and 99 percent list a phone. In Santa Cruz the website share is 84 percent.
