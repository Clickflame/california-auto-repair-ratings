What To Do With These Numbers

Shops here have fewer reviews than usual. The middle shop has 44 against 53 statewide, so look closely at the count before you trust the stars.

In Oakland, 74 percent of rated shops hold 4.5 stars or more. So a high score alone will not narrow your list much. Look for a shop with a strong score and a deep review count, then call and ask for an estimate.
