What To Do With These Numbers

The middle shop has 54 reviews, close to the state median of 53. Review depth here is about normal for California.

In Los Angeles, 74 percent of rated shops hold 4.5 stars or more. So a high score alone will not narrow your list much. Look for a shop with a strong score and a deep review count, then call and ask for an estimate.
