What To Do With These Numbers

Shops here have more reviews than usual. The middle shop has 67 against 53 statewide, so the stars rest on more voices.

In Long Beach, 66 percent of rated shops hold 4.5 stars or more. So a high score alone will not narrow your list much. Look for a shop with a strong score and a deep review count, then call and ask for an estimate.
