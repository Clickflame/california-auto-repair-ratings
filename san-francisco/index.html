What To Do With These Numbers

Review counts run high in San Francisco. A median of 70 beats the state median of 53.

In San Francisco, 82 percent of rated shops hold 4.5 stars or more. So a high score alone will not narrow your list much. Look for a shop with a strong score and a deep review count, then call and ask for an estimate.
